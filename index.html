Before You Ask

Any rider who shows up with intent. No racing background needed. Just a roadworthy bike and respect for the group.

Priority access to group rides, member-only events, curated route drops, and first access to Driftline gear releases.

Every ride is graded by difficulty and average speed, so you know exactly what you're signing up for before you clip in.

Pick a ride, reserve your spot, show up at the meeting point. Spots are limited; late sign-ups don't get a second chance.

How to joinThe Community

Apply

Submit your details and tell us how you ride. Your pace, your experience, the kind of routes you want. No résumé, no racing history required - just honest answers.

01

Get Approved

Every application is reviewed by a real person. We keep numbers small on purpose, so each rider added fits the standard already on the road. You'll hear back within 48 hours.

02

Pick Your Ride

Browse upcoming rides graded by distance, difficulty, and average speed. Choose one that matches your level, check the meeting point, and reserve your spot before it fills.

03

Show Up

Arrive at the start, bike checked, ready to go. Meet the group, clip in, and ride. From here, you're not a visitor anymore - you're part of what Driftline is.
